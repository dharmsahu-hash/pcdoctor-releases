# PCDoctor by KnowledgeWala — downloads

PCDoctor explains in plain words why your Windows PC is slow, hot or full, and helps you fix it safely. Everything stays on your PC: no account, no tracking, no ads.

## Download

Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `PCDoctor_<version>_x64-setup.exe`. Double-click it, then click **Next**, **Install**, **Finish**. No admin password needed.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Update

Download the newest installer from **Releases** and run it over the old one. Your history, My Library, AI files and conversation are kept.

If you switch on **Check for updates** in PCDoctor's Settings, PCDoctor asks this page once a day whether a newer version exists. It sends nothing about you or your PC.
